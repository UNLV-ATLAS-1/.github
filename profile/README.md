# UNLV ATLAS-1

UNLV ATLAS-1 is an independent research project at the University of Nevada, Las Vegas, dedicated to making high-altitude ballooning tests and CubeSat development more accessible and attainable within the Nevada region.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to design, build, and launch a CubeSat from the state of Nevada. Our goal is not only to showcase innovation but also to strengthen Nevada's presence in the aerospace industry.

## Projects

### MOSS Kit

The MOSS Kit (Modular Outreach Starter Satellite) is a telemetry platform designed for accessible high-altitude balloon flights. It includes a flight payload, ground station, and telemetry framework.

The MOSS Kit repository can be found at: [unlv-atlas-1/mosskit](https://github.com/UNLV-ATLAS-1/MOSSKIT)
